Chroma — Quick Guide & Shortcuts

Press F1 anytime to toggle this guide. Press Esc or click outside to close. Tip: You can also drag & drop images directly onto the canvas.

Toolbar Tools

Move
Reposition the active layer freely across the canvas.
Resize
Scale the active layer using corner and edge handles.
Crop
Drag a selection rectangle and click Apply Crop in the Canvas panel.
Hand
Pan around the viewport without moving any layer content.
Brush
Paint freehand strokes with adjustable size, hardness, and opacity.
Eraser
Erase pixels smoothly with soft edges or hard ink precision.
Fill
Flood-fill contiguous areas with the primary color.
Picker
Sample any pixel color from the composite canvas.
Text / Shapes
Place custom text, lines, rectangles, and ellipses.

Top Bar & Panels

Open
Import an image file (PNG, JPEG, WebP) as a new layer.
Export
Download the flattened composition in PNG, JPEG, or WebP format.
Undo / Redo
Step backward or forward through your edit history.
Layer
Create a new transparent layer above the active layer.
Fit
Center and reset the canvas view in the workspace.
Filters
Preview live brightness, contrast, blur, sharpen, and pixelate effects before applying.
Layers
Reorder, duplicate, rename, toggle visibility, and adjust layer opacity/blend modes.
Canvas
Change document dimensions or commit an active crop area.

Quick Workflow

  1. Click Open (or drag & drop an image onto the stage) to load your base image.
  2. Use Move (M) or Resize (R) to position and scale the layer.
  3. Adjust colors or apply convolution filters in the Filters panel, then click Apply.
  4. Add a new layer to paint with Brush (B), erase details with Eraser (E), or add labels with Text (T).
  5. Choose your target format (PNG/JPEG/WebP) and click Export to save your work.
